Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

GeoMark ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles your information.

Information We Collect

The application may access:

  • Camera (to capture photos).
  • Location (to display GPS coordinates and address on photos).
  • Storage (to save generated photos on your device).

How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used only to provide the application's features, including:

  • Capturing photos.
